YELLOW MUD CERAMICS FACTORY

ARCHITECTURE
May, 2023

A ceramics workshop sitting atop rice paddy terraces within a peninsula along the Yangtze. It embodies elements of distributed and scattered facilities within the city of Yichang.

Encompassing the traditions of making ceramics while also reclaiming the silt deposited at the bottom of the Yangtze to reform into clay. Collaborated with Durdona Rustomova.
