فاطمة يوسف الهاشمي

Fatma Y. Alhashimi is an Emirati multidisciplinary artist, conceptualizing and iterating through words, illustrations, fabrications, and design. She projects herself through methodical notions that lean towards whimsicality and emotion to drive her concepts and fictions.

She holds an MPS in Interactive Telecommunications Program from Tisch School of the Arts at New York University, a BFA in Architectural Design and a Minor in Sustainable Cities from Parsons School of Design at The Newschool.
