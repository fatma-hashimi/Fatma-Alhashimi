TIDAL RITES
FABRICATION P.COMP
Jan, 2026

A location-specific artifact entwined with the tide station at The Battery's pier. Enclosed within is a small computer that queries NOAA's tidal API, translating the harbor's rise and fall into light. This vessel represents low tide as a dim blob at the bottom and high tide by swelling to its brim. It holds no water, but reflects the harbor's rhythm. Think of it as a living, landlocked echo, bound to the shore even through distance.
